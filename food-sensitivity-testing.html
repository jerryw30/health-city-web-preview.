Understanding the Science: Allergy vs. Sensitivity

It is common to have a "normal" allergy test yet still feel unwell after eating. This is because your immune system has different ways of responding to triggers.

IgE Mediated (Allergy): This is a classic, immediate reaction (like a peanut allergy) that causes hives, swelling, or respiratory distress.

IgG Mediated (Sensitivity): These are delayed reactions that can appear hours or even days after consumption. Because the response is slow, it is nearly impossible to identify the trigger without specialized testing. These sensitivities are often the "hidden" drivers behind chronic brain fog, joint pain, and digestive distress.
