Why "Normal" Labs Aren't Always "Optimal"

The standard healthcare model often only tests TSH (Thyroid Stimulating Hormone). However, your thyroid health is a complex relay race involving your brain, your thyroid gland, your liver, and your gut.

We investigate the full thyroid panel to find the "why" behind your symptoms:

T4 to T3 Conversion: Your thyroid produces T4 (inactive), but your body needs T3 (active) for energy. Stress and high cortisol can "block" this conversion.

Reverse T3 (The Brake Pedal): When you are under a heavy physiological load, your body may produce Reverse T3 to intentionally slow your metabolism down.

Thyroid Antibodies: We screen for Hashimoto’s Thyroiditis to see if an underlying immune flare is the root cause of your fluctuations.

Personalized Medication Options: Beyond Levothyroxine

Many women find that standard synthetic T4 (Levothyroxine/Synthroid) doesn't fully resolve their symptoms. This is often because their bodies struggle to convert that T4 into the active T3 they need to feel vibrant.

As a Naturopathic Doctor with prescribing authority in Ontario, I offer a more nuanced approach to hormone replacement via our secure virtual clinic:

Desiccated Thyroid Hormone (NDT): A naturally derived, porcine-source thyroid medication that contains a combination of both T4 and T3. For many women, the addition of T3 is the "missing piece" that restores their mental clarity and metabolic fire.

Customized Combination Therapy: Tailoring your treatment to match your unique biochemistry, rather than a one-size-fits-all dose.

Nutrient Co-Factors: Ensuring you have nutrients like Tyrosine, Selenium, Zinc, and Iodine required for your thyroid hormones to actually work at the cellular level.

Frequently Asked Questions

"Can I switch from Synthroid to Desiccated Thyroid?" Patients can try to transition completely but often benefit by supplementing their T4 meds with desiccated thyroid. We can support this transition strategically and monitor your labs closely.

"Do you prescribe thyroid medication in Ontario?" Yes. As an ND with the appropriate licensing, I can prescribe desiccated thyroid hormones to residents of Ontario.

"Why is my hair still falling out if my TSH is normal?" Hair loss can be tied to "sub-clinical" thyroid issues, low iron (ferritin), various nutrient deficiencies, abnormal hormones, and stressful events. We look at all potential causes and contributors to get your hair—and your energy—back.
