Virtual Hormonal & Metabolic Health Support in Ontario

Understand your symptoms. Explore the factors influencing your health. Find a way forward.

When your energy, mood, weight or overall wellbeing changes, it can be difficult to know where to start. You may be feeling persistently tired, struggling with changes in body composition, noticing shifts in your libido or experiencing symptoms that don't seem to have a clear explanation.

Hormones and metabolic health influence many aspects of how we feel and function. Thyroid health, blood sugar regulation, sex hormones, nutrition, sleep and stress can all play a role.

In my virtual naturopathic practice, I work with men and women to explore these interconnected factors, make sense of relevant health information and develop an individualized approach to support their health.

Signs Your Hormonal & Metabolic Health May Need Attention

Hormonal and metabolic changes can look different from person to person. Some concerns that may benefit from a closer look include:

For Men

Changes in sexual health: Reduced libido or changes in erectile function.

Energy & motivation: Persistent fatigue, reduced motivation or difficulty concentrating.

Body composition: Changes in abdominal weight, muscle mass or exercise recovery.

Physical wellbeing: Changes in sleep, hair or overall vitality.

For Women

Menstrual health: Irregular periods, significant PMS or changes in cycle patterns.

Perimenopause: Changes in sleep, mood, energy, menstrual cycles or body composition.

Metabolic health: Changes in weight, appetite, blood sugar regulation or energy.

Skin & hair: Changes in skin, hair or nails that may warrant further investigation.

These symptoms can have many possible contributors. A thorough look at your health history and individual circumstances helps determine what may be worth exploring.

Understanding Your Hormonal & Metabolic Health

There isn't one test or hormone that explains everything. Depending on your symptoms, health history and goals, we may consider several areas:

Thyroid & Metabolic Health

Thyroid function, blood sugar regulation, insulin sensitivity and other metabolic markers can influence energy, body composition and overall wellbeing.

Sex Hormones

For men, this may include testosterone and related markers. For women, it may involve estrogen, progesterone and the hormonal changes associated with menstrual cycles and perimenopause.

Stress, Sleep & Recovery

Stress, sleep patterns and daily demands can influence hormonal and metabolic health. Understanding these factors helps us consider the bigger picture rather than focusing on lab results alone.

Nutrition & Nutrient Status

Dietary patterns, nutrient intake and individual nutritional needs can influence metabolic function and overall health. We consider these alongside your symptoms and relevant clinical findings.

A Personalized Approach to Your Health

My approach combines nutritional science, clinical experience and an understanding of how different aspects of health interact. Recommendations are based on your individual needs—not a standard hormone protocol.

Depending on your circumstances, your care may include:

A closer look at your health history: We'll review your symptoms, health history, lifestyle, priorities and any existing lab results.

Testing when appropriate: Bloodwork or selected additional investigations may help provide useful information. Testing is chosen based on your individual situation, rather than using every available test.

Personalized nutrition & lifestyle support: We'll explore practical strategies related to nutrition, movement, sleep and stress management.

Ongoing guidance: Your plan can evolve as we review your progress, discuss what's working and identify any next steps.

The goal is to help you better understand your health and make informed, sustainable changes.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can hormone levels be normal even when I'm experiencing symptoms?

Yes. Lab results need to be interpreted in the context of your symptoms, health history and the reason testing was ordered. A result within a laboratory reference range doesn't always answer every question, but symptoms alone don't establish a hormone imbalance either. We'll consider the full picture when discussing your results and next steps.

Do you offer hormone testing for men and women?

Yes. Depending on your concerns, testing may include bloodwork to assess thyroid function, metabolic markers and relevant sex hormones. Other testing options may be considered when appropriate. Not everyone needs specialized hormone testing.

Can I work with you if I'm already seeing a medical doctor or specialist?

Absolutely. Naturopathic care can complement your existing healthcare. With your consent, relevant lab results and medical information can help inform our discussions. I encourage patients to continue working with their family doctor and other healthcare providers.

Are virtual appointments available across Ontario?

Yes. Consultations are offered virtually for patients throughout Ontario, so you can access care from home.
