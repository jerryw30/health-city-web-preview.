Period Problems & Menstrual Health Support in Ontario

Painful, heavy or unpredictable periods shouldn't have to dictate your life.

Do you plan your schedule around your period? Struggle with painful cramps, heavy bleeding or unpredictable cycles? Perhaps you've been told your symptoms are normal, even though they affect your ability to work, exercise or enjoy everyday life.

Your menstrual cycle can offer valuable information about your health. Changes in your period deserve attention, especially when symptoms interfere with your quality of life.

Through virtual naturopathic care, I support women across Ontario in exploring menstrual health concerns, understanding potential contributing factors and developing an individualized approach to their wellbeing.

What Period Problems Can Tell Us About Your Health

Menstrual symptoms can have different underlying causes. Understanding your cycle, health history and symptoms is an important starting point.

1. Painful Periods

Some discomfort during menstruation can be common, but debilitating pain is not something you should simply have to endure.

Painful periods can be associated with several factors, including increased prostaglandin activity, endometriosis, adenomyosis and other gynecological conditions.

Our approach may include exploring nutritional status, digestive health, lifestyle factors and relevant medical investigations.

If you experience significant period pain, it's important to discuss medical assessment with your healthcare provider.

Learn more about Endometriosis Support.

2. Heavy or Prolonged Periods

Heavy bleeding, prolonged periods or frequent spotting can affect your energy, daily activities and iron levels.

Possible contributors include hormonal changes, ovulatory irregularities, fibroids, adenomyosis and other gynecological conditions.

Depending on your symptoms, we may explore menstrual patterns, nutritional status, iron levels and relevant hormonal or medical factors.

Very heavy bleeding, particularly when accompanied by dizziness, faintness or weakness, warrants prompt medical attention.

3. Irregular or Absent Periods

Are your cycles unpredictable, unusually long or missing altogether?

Irregular periods can be associated with changes in ovulation, thyroid function, metabolic health, stress, significant changes in weight or exercise, and reproductive conditions such as polyendocrine metabolic ovarian syndrome (PMOS), formerly known as PCOS.

Understanding your cycle patterns and health history can help identify which areas may warrant further investigation and support.

Explore our PMOS Support and Perimenopause Support pages.

A Personalized Approach to Menstrual Health

There isn't one explanation or solution for every period problem. Your care begins with understanding your symptoms, health history and goals.

Depending on your needs, our work may include:

Hormonal & Metabolic Health

Exploring relevant hormonal, thyroid and metabolic factors that may influence your menstrual cycle.

Nutrition & Digestive Health

Reviewing dietary patterns, nutrient intake, iron status and digestive concerns that may be relevant to your overall health.

Stress, Sleep & Nervous System Health

Considering how stress, sleep quality, recovery and lifestyle may influence menstrual health.

Individualized Testing

When appropriate, laboratory testing may provide additional information. This can include bloodwork and selected at-home testing, depending on your symptoms and clinical picture. Testing is recommended when it may help inform your care—not simply because it's available.

How Virtual Naturopathic Care Works

You don't need to live in Toronto to access care.

Initial consultation: We discuss your menstrual history, symptoms, health background and goals.

Personalized recommendations: We identify relevant areas to explore and develop an individualized care plan.

Testing, if appropriate: When indicated, bloodwork can be arranged through available laboratories in Ontario, and selected at-home test kits may be considered.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are painful periods normal?

Some menstrual discomfort is common, but severe pain that interferes with daily life deserves medical attention. Painful periods can sometimes be associated with conditions such as endometriosis or adenomyosis, which may require further investigation.

Can naturopathic care help with irregular periods?

Naturopathic care may support aspects of nutrition, lifestyle and overall health while exploring factors that could be contributing to irregular cycles. The appropriate approach depends on the underlying cause, and medical assessment may be needed.

Can I work with you virtually if I live outside Toronto?

Yes. I offer virtual naturopathic consultations to women throughout Ontario.

Can you help if I want to come off hormonal birth control?

Yes. We can discuss your reasons for stopping, what to expect as your natural cycle resumes, and how to support your health during the transition. There is no guaranteed way to prevent symptoms from returning, but understanding your menstrual history and having a plan can help.
