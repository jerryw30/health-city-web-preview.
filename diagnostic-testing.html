A deeper look at your health

Routine bloodwork and conventional medical testing play an important role in healthcare. But they don't always provide a complete picture of every aspect of how your body is functioning.

Depending on your health history, symptoms and goals, additional testing may offer valuable insight into areas that aren't routinely assessed through standard investigations. These tests can help us explore specific aspects of digestive health, hormone function, nutrient status, metabolic health and other physiological processes.

Sometimes, the information helps us investigate questions that remain unanswered. In other cases, it provides a more detailed understanding of your current health and helps guide decisions around nutrition, lifestyle and preventative care.

At Health & the City, I take an individualized approach to testing. Rather than recommending every available option, I consider which investigations may offer meaningful information for your particular circumstances. The goal is to look beyond isolated symptoms, understand the bigger picture and use relevant findings to help inform your healthcare decisions.

How we decide what to test

Three questions we ask before ordering anything

1. Will it answer a real question?

Every test is tied to a specific symptom, risk factor, or decision you’re facing. Nothing is ordered “just in case.”

2. Will it change the plan?

If every possible result would lead to the same next step, testing adds cost without adding clarity. When that’s the case, we’ll tell you.

3. Can we interpret it responsibly?

Before you test, we talk through what the research supports, how timing and medications can affect results, and what the test can’t tell us. A number is never treated as more meaningful than it is.
