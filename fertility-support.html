Fertility & Preconception Support in Toronto

Thoughtful, personalized support for your fertility journey.

Whether you're preparing for pregnancy, navigating fertility challenges, or going through IVF, you deserve care that considers the whole picture.

Trying to conceive can be physically and emotionally demanding. You may have questions about your hormones, nutrition, menstrual cycle, or what else you can do to support your health along the way.

I understand how personal this journey can be. It took me 3.5 years and significant medical intervention to conceive my daughter. Later, approaching 40, with stage IV endometriosis and only one fallopian tube, I conceived my son naturally. At 42, I went on to conceive my third healthy child naturally and quickly.

Having experienced very different fertility journeys myself, I understand that there isn't one path to pregnancy. I bring that perspective, along with my training in naturopathic medicine and nutritional sciences, to help women navigate their own fertility journeys with greater understanding, confidence and support.

How Can Naturopathic Fertility Support Help?

Fertility is influenced by many factors, including reproductive health, nutrition, metabolic health, lifestyle and overall wellbeing. My approach is individualized and works alongside appropriate medical and fertility care.

1. Preconception & Pregnancy Preparation

Planning for pregnancy is an opportunity to build a strong foundation for your health and a future pregnancy.

Our work may include:

Nutritional support: Reviewing dietary patterns, nutrient intake and prenatal supplementation.

Hormonal and menstrual health: Exploring cycle patterns, ovulation and relevant hormonal concerns.

Lifestyle considerations: Discussing sleep, stress, movement and environmental exposures.

Preconception planning: Identifying areas where additional support or medical investigation may be appropriate.

Whether you're hoping to conceive soon or planning several months ahead, we can create an approach that reflects your individual needs.

2. Support Alongside IVF & Fertility Treatments

Going through IUI, IVF or egg freezing can involve a significant physical, emotional and financial commitment.

Naturopathic care can provide additional support alongside your fertility clinic, with attention to:

Nutrition and nutrient intake

Sleep, stress and emotional wellbeing

Digestive health and overall wellness

Reviewing supplements for safety and potential interactions with fertility medications

Supporting general health throughout treatment and recovery

3. Understanding Fertility Challenges

When pregnancy isn't happening as expected, it can be frustrating to receive reassuring test results without a clear explanation for your experience.

Depending on your history and concerns, we may explore:

Menstrual cycle patterns and ovulation

Thyroid and metabolic health

Nutritional status and potential nutrient deficiencies

Digestive health and overall wellbeing

Relevant medical investigations and previous fertility testing

Additional testing may be considered when appropriate, but it isn't necessary for everyone. The goal is to understand your health picture and identify meaningful next steps—not to order more tests for the sake of testing.

Fertility Support for Endometriosis & PMOS (Formerly PCOS)

Conditions such as endometriosis and polyendocrine metabolic ovarian syndrome (PMOS), formerly known as PCOS, can influence menstrual health, ovulation and fertility.

If you're navigating either condition, your care should reflect your individual symptoms, health history and reproductive goals.

My approach may include nutritional support, attention to metabolic and hormonal health, and coordination with your medical providers where appropriate.

Frequently Asked Questions

When should I see a naturopathic doctor for fertility support?

You can start before trying to conceive, while actively trying, or alongside fertility treatment. If you're experiencing difficulty conceiving, don't delay a medical fertility assessment while pursuing complementary support. In general, fertility evaluation is recommended after 12 months of trying if you're under 35, after 6 months if you're 35 or older, and sooner when there are known reproductive concerns.

Can you work alongside my fertility clinic?

Yes. Naturopathic care can complement your medical fertility care. With your consent, relevant information can be shared with your healthcare team.

Is fertility support covered by insurance?

Many extended health plans include naturopathic services, but coverage varies by provider and individual policy. Check your plan for details.
