Understanding Endometriosis

More than just painful periods.

Endometriosis is a chronic inflammatory condition in which tissue similar to the lining of the uterus grows outside the uterus.

It can cause a range of symptoms, and no two people's experiences are exactly alike.

Common symptoms and associated concerns include:

  • Severe or painful menstrual periods
  • Pelvic pain, including pain outside menstruation
  • Pain with ovulation or sexual activity
  • Persistent bloating and digestive discomfort
  • Changes in bowel or bladder function
  • Fatigue and disrupted sleep
  • Fertility challenges
  • The emotional and psychological impact of living with chronic pain

Some people experience severe symptoms, while others have few or no obvious symptoms.

Endometriosis can also take years to diagnose. Your symptoms deserve attention even when previous investigations haven't provided clear answers.

Understanding your experience is an important first step toward finding the support that's right for you.

Looking Beyond Your Diagnosis

Endometriosis care should consider the whole picture.

Medical and surgical treatments are important components of endometriosis management. Depending on your circumstances, these may include hormonal therapies, pain management, surgery and fertility-related care.

But managing a complex, chronic condition can involve more than one type of support.

Nutrition, digestive health, sleep, movement, stress and overall wellbeing may also deserve attention as part of an individualized care approach.

This is where naturopathic medicine may complement your existing healthcare.

Rather than assuming there is one underlying issue responsible for everything, we take time to understand your health history, identify relevant concerns and explore appropriate supportive strategies.

The goal is not to replace your medical care. It's to help you navigate your health with a more comprehensive perspective.

Our Approach to Endometriosis Support

A comprehensive, individualized approach.

Endometriosis is complex. Your care should reflect that.

Depending on your individual needs, naturopathic support may explore the following areas:

1. Hormonal Health

Explore menstrual patterns, hormonal symptoms and factors relevant to your overall reproductive health.

Where appropriate, we consider the role of estrogen-related pathways and how hormonal health fits into your broader clinical picture.

2. Nutrition & Inflammatory Health

Explore nutritional patterns and dietary factors that may influence overall health and wellbeing.

We focus on practical, sustainable nutrition strategies rather than restrictive diets or promises of an anti-inflammatory cure.

3. Digestive Health

Bloating, bowel changes and digestive discomfort can be significant concerns for people living with endometriosis.

We consider your digestive symptoms, relevant health history and whether further assessment or collaboration with other healthcare professionals is appropriate.

4. Stress, Sleep & Nervous System Support

Living with persistent pain can take a toll on sleep, emotional wellbeing and everyday functioning.

We explore practical strategies to support sleep, stress management and quality of life without suggesting that stress causes endometriosis.

5. Individualized Nutritional & Supplement Support

Where appropriate, we consider nutritional status, potential deficiencies, medication interactions and the evidence for specific supplements.

Recommendations are individualized rather than based on a generic endometriosis supplement protocol.

6. Collaborative, Ongoing Care

Your health needs may change over time.

We consider how naturopathic support can complement your existing medical care, including conversations around surgery, fertility, symptom management and other treatment decisions.

This is thoughtful, individualized support—not a one-size-fits-all protocol or a promise of a quick fix.

Frequently Asked Questions About Endometriosis & Naturopathic Care

Can a naturopath cure endometriosis?

No. Endometriosis is a chronic condition, and naturopathic care does not cure or remove endometriosis lesions. Naturopathic support may address relevant aspects of nutrition, digestive health, pain, fatigue and overall wellbeing.

Can a naturopath help with endometriosis pain?

A naturopathic doctor may help explore supportive approaches to overall health and symptom management. However, evidence for specific naturopathic interventions as treatments for endometriosis-related pain remains limited. Pain management should be discussed with an appropriately qualified healthcare professional, and naturopathic care should complement rather than replace appropriate medical treatment.

Should I see a naturopath after endometriosis surgery?

You may choose to explore naturopathic support as part of your broader recovery and ongoing health care.

How do hormones affect endometriosis?

Endometriosis is an estrogen-dependent condition involving complex hormonal, inflammatory and immune processes. Hormonal treatments are among the established medical management options. Understanding menstrual patterns and hormonal symptoms may also be relevant when discussing your broader health with your healthcare team.

Can I work with Dr. Natalie if I live outside Ontario?

Personalized naturopathic appointments through Health & the City are available to eligible clients in Ontario. If you live elsewhere, you can still access our educational articles, free resources and endometriosis-specific digital products, without needing to book an appointment.
