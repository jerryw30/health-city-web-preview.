About Dr. Natalie

Meet Dr. Natalie Bozinovski ND, MSc.

I believe women deserve to feel heard, understand their bodies, and have more options when it comes to their health.

I'm Dr. Natalie Bozinovski—a Naturopathic Doctor, Master of Nutritional Sciences, published author and speaker.

I'm also a woman who knows firsthand how frustrating it can be to live with symptoms that affect your quality of life, especially when you don't feel like you're getting the answers you're looking for.

In my Ontario practice, I work with women who want to understand what's going on in their bodies and take a more proactive approach to their health. Whether you're dealing with painful periods, endometriosis, hormonal changes, digestive issues, fatigue or simply feeling unlike yourself, I want to help you connect the dots and find a way forward.

My approach brings together science, clinical experience and a genuine understanding that your health needs to work with your life—not against it.

My Story: Why Women's Health Is Personal

I didn't choose women's health simply because it was an area of interest. I found my way here through my own experience.

I've lived with stage IV endometriosis for nearly 20 years. I know what it's like to be paralyzed on the floor in agony, to worry about your future fertility, and to feel dismissed when you're looking for answers.

I was 22, still in university, when I was told I should consider having a baby immediately because there was a good chance I would not be able to later on. I was shocked. I was in no position to have children at that age

That experience changed the direction of my life. I wanted to understand the science behind what I was experiencing and explore how nutrition, lifestyle and naturopathic medicine could fit into a broader approach to women's health.

My journey to motherhood was far from straightforward. It took 3.5 years and significant medical intervention to conceive my daughter. Later, approaching 40, with stage IV endometriosis and only one fallopian tube, I conceived my son naturally. At 42, I conceived my third child naturally and quickly.

I share this because I know how much uncertainty can come with a fertility journey. My experience is not a promise of what is possible for someone else, and every woman's circumstances are different. But it has shaped how I show up for my patients: with empathy, curiosity and respect for the many different paths to motherhood—and to better health.

It also inspired the Natural Endo Movement. I want women to have access to better information, feel more confident asking questions and know that they deserve to be active participants in their healthcare.

This experience taught me that when we restore our physiological reserves, we unlock our capacity to show up for our families and our biggest goals.

Life Beyond the Clinic

I believe a healthy lifestyle shouldn't feel like a second job.

Yes, making changes can take some effort, especially at the beginning. But ultimately, it's about finding a rhythm that supports your actual life—not creating another list of things you're supposed to do perfectly.

When I'm not working, you'll usually find me:

With my family: Enjoying the beautiful chaos of motherhood with my husband, our three children and our dog, Luna. They keep me grounded and remind me why feeling well matters.

Investing in growth: I'm a lifelong student of personal development and the connection between the mind and body. I'm fascinated by how our thoughts, feelings, habits and physical wellbeing influence one another.

In motion: Strength training and long walks are non-negotiables for me. Movement helps me clear my head, shift my energy and show up more fully in the rest of my life.

In the kitchen: I love experimenting with recipes. For me, food is a blend of science and creativity—and there's usually almond flour and a good playlist involved.

I'm not interested in chasing perfection. I'm interested in building a foundation that lets you enjoy your life and have the energy to do the things that matter to you.

How I Work With You

I see my role as a partner in your health journey. I bring the science, ask questions, look at the bigger picture and help you make sense of your options. You bring your experience, priorities and goals. We put the pieces together.

What you can expect:

A thoughtful, evidence-informed approach: I draw on clinical research, nutritional science and your individual health history—not one-size-fits-all wellness trends.

A space where you feel heard: Your symptoms and concerns matter. You shouldn't have to convince me that what you're experiencing is affecting your life.

A plan that makes sense for you: We look at what may be contributing to your symptoms and which nutrition, lifestyle or testing strategies may be appropriate. Not every person needs every test or supplement.

Care that fits your life: I offer virtual consultations across Ontario, so you can make your health a priority without adding another commute to your day.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What can a naturopathic doctor help with?

Naturopathic doctors take an individualized approach to health, using nutrition, lifestyle counselling, botanical medicine and other therapies within their scope of practice. In my practice, I focus on women's hormonal and menstrual health, digestive concerns, fatigue, stress and proactive wellness. The right approach depends on your health history, symptoms and goals.

2. What happens during an initial naturopathic consultation?

Your first appointment is an opportunity to tell your story. We'll review your health history, current concerns, symptoms, lifestyle and goals. Together, we'll identify priorities and discuss whether further assessment, testing or a personalized care plan may be appropriate.

3. Do I need to stop seeing my family doctor or specialist?

Absolutely not. Naturopathic care can complement your existing healthcare. I value collaboration with family doctors and specialists and encourage patients to maintain their relationships with their conventional healthcare providers.

4. Do you recommend supplements or special diets?

Not automatically. Recommendations are based on your individual needs, health history and goals. When appropriate, we may discuss nutrition changes, supplements or other lifestyle strategies. My goal is to make recommendations that are practical, purposeful and sustainable—not give you an endless list of things to buy or avoid.

5. Do I need testing before my first appointment?

No. You don't need to arrive with a collection of lab results or complete testing beforehand. If you have recent bloodwork or relevant medical records, you're welcome to share them. We'll discuss whether any additional testing may be useful once I understand your health history and concerns.

6. Do you offer virtual naturopathic appointments in Ontario?

Yes. I offer virtual consultations for patients across Ontario. You can meet with me from the comfort of your home, without needing to travel to a clinic.

7. Does insurance cover naturopathic appointments?

Many extended health benefit plans include coverage for naturopathic services, but coverage varies by provider and policy. I recommend checking your plan for your annual allowance, eligible services and any referral requirements.

8. How do I know if we're a good fit?

The best place to start is by learning more about my approach and the areas of health I work with. If you're unsure whether naturopathic care is right for your needs, you can book a complimentary discovery call to ask questions and see whether working together feels like a good fit.
