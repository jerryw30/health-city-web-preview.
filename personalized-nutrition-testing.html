The Science of Personalized Nutrition

Nutrigenomics is the study of how your genes and your nutrition interact. Through a non-invasive at-home saliva test shipped directly to your door in Ontario, we map the biochemical pathways that dictate your health:

1. Genetic Nutrient Requirements

Not everyone utilizes vitamins the same way. We look at your genetic predisposition for deficiencies in Vitamin D, B12, Iron, and Omega-3s. This allows us to move past general supplementation and target what your body requires more for optimal cellular function.

2. Metabolic & Cardiovascular Signaling

Your DNA dictates how your system responds to dietary staples. We analyze your genetic sensitivity to:

Caffeine & Stimulants: Identifying if you are a slow or fast metabolizer—essential for addressing sleep quality and burnout.

Sodium & Alcohol: Understanding your genetic resilience to systemic inflammation and cardiovascular stress.

Fats & Carbohydrates: Seeing how your blueprint influences insulin sensitivity and metabolic efficiency.

3. Performance, Recovery & Injury Risk

For high-capacity individuals and athletes, we map the markers that dictate muscle power, aerobic capacity, and recovery time. We also identify your genetic risk for tendon or ligament issues, allowing us to build a proactive strategy for injury prevention.

4. Hormonal Health & Antioxidant Capacity

We look at how your genetics impact folate metabolism and your body’s natural detoxification pathways. This is foundational for supporting reproductive health, cycle regularity, and long-term vitality.

A Strategic Roadmap to Biochemical Optimization
