SIBO Breath Testing & Clinical Strategy in Ontario

Stop Managing the Bloat. Start Addressing the Root Cause.

The most common signs of SIBO include:

The "SIBO Belly": Significant bloating that typically starts 30 to 90 minutes after eating, often worsening as the day progresses.

Altered Bowel Habits: Depending on the gas produced, this usually manifests as either diarrhea, or constipation but could be mixed.

The Fiber Paradox: Unlike general digestive issues, SIBO symptoms often worsen with increased fiber, prebiotics, or standard probiotics, as these feed the overgrowth.

Nutrient Depletion: Chronic malabsorption of Vitamin B12 and Iron, often leading to persistent anemia or fatigue despite supplementation.

Systemic Flares: Beyond the gut, SIBO is a frequent driver of brain fog, skin issues (like Rosacea), and the inflammatory cycles seen in "Endo-belly."

In my virtual naturopathic practice, we use the clinical gold standard: 3-Hour Lactulose Breath Testing. By measuring the specific gases produced by these bacteria, we can identify the type and severity of the overgrowth and build a precise, data-driven roadmap to restore your foundation.

The Science of the SIBO Breath Test

Digestion is a rhythmic process. When bacteria interfere with the small intestine, they ferment food prematurely, creating gases that lead to systemic inflammation and localized discomfort.

Through a non-invasive at-home breath test shipped directly to your door anywhere in Ontario, we measure two primary markers:

1. Hydrogen (H2) Production

Hydrogen-dominant SIBO is often associated with rapid transit time and reactive bloating. Identifying this allows us to target the overgrowth with specific botanical or antimicrobial agents tailored to this bacterial profile.

2. Methane (CH4) Production

Methane-producing organisms (methanogens) are often the "hidden" drivers behind chronic constipation and metabolic plateaus. Methane can slow down the entire digestive tract, making it harder to clear hormones and toxins effectively.

Why a 3-Hour Test Matters

Short-duration breath tests often miss overgrowths that occur further down the digestive tract. In my practice, we utilize a 3-hour collection window to ensure we capture the full transit of the lactulose through the small intestine. This provides a more accurate "map" of where the bacteria are located, which is essential for a successful treatment strategy.
