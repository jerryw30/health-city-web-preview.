Gut Health & Digestive Support in Ontario

Persistent bloating, unpredictable digestion or ongoing discomfort? Let's take a closer look.

If you've spent years managing bloating, constipation, diarrhea, IBS symptoms or food-related discomfort, you know how much digestive issues can affect everyday life.

You may have tried eliminating foods, taking probiotics or following generic gut health advice without getting the clarity or support you need.

In my virtual naturopathic practice, I take an individualized, evidence-informed approach to digestive health. Together, we'll explore your symptoms, health history, nutrition and lifestyle to identify relevant contributing factors and develop a plan that makes sense for you.

Understanding the Factors That Influence Gut Health

Digestive health is influenced by more than what you eat. The way your body breaks down food, absorbs nutrients, maintains its microbial environment and communicates with the nervous system can all affect how you feel.

Depending on your symptoms and health history, we may explore:

Digestive Function & Nutrient Absorption

Healthy digestion relies on several interconnected processes, including adequate stomach acid, digestive enzyme activity, bile production and release, and intestinal function. These processes help break down food, absorb nutrients and support regular bowel movements.

We may explore whether factors involving stomach function, pancreatic enzymes, gallbladder or liver health, and nutrient absorption are relevant to your digestive symptoms.

IBS, Bloating & Digestive Discomfort

Bloating, abdominal discomfort, constipation and diarrhea can have several contributing factors, including altered gut motility, visceral sensitivity, food intolerances and gut–brain interactions. Understanding your symptom patterns is an important first step in determining what may be contributing.

SIBO & Microbial Health

Changes in the gut microbiome and small intestinal bacterial overgrowth (SIBO) may contribute to bloating, gas and changes in bowel habits in some individuals. When appropriate, breath testing or selected stool investigations may provide additional information.

Food-Related Symptoms & Nutritional Health

If certain foods seem to trigger symptoms, we'll explore your dietary patterns, potential food intolerances, nutritional intake and whether further investigation may be appropriate. The goal is to understand your individual needs without unnecessary dietary restriction.

The Gut–Brain Connection

Your digestive system and nervous system are closely connected. Stress, sleep and nervous system activity can influence gut motility, digestive comfort and sensitivity. These factors are important considerations when developing a comprehensive approach to digestive health.
